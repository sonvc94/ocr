# Official specimen images (見本)

Sample images published by Japanese government bodies; the personal data printed on them is fictitious.
Downloaded 2026-10-28, re-encoded to JPEG (alpha flattened on white). Residence card specimens are in `../isa/`
(Immigration Services Agency).

| File | Document | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `my_number_card.jpg` | マイナンバーカード, front (`マイナンバーカード表面（見本）`) | Kobe city — https://www.city.kobe.lg.jp/a53715/mynumbercard.html (`/images/39998/card1.png`) |
| `driver_license.jpg` | 運転免許証, front; **cropped** to the card (the original has explanatory arrows and text on the right) | Kanagawa Prefectural Police — https://www.police.pref.kanagawa.jp/tetsuzuki/menkyo/mes83080.html (`83080_01.png`) |
| `passport_2025.jpg` | 旅券 identity page, 2025 passport (applications from 2025-03-24) | Kanagawa Prefecture — https://www.pref.kanagawa.jp/docs/h6d/passport/aboutpass.html (`2025_mibunjiko.png`) |
| `passport_2020.jpg` | 旅券 identity page, earlier passport | same page (`mibunjiko.png`) |
| `health_insurance_shikaku_kakuninsho.jpg` | 国民健康保険 資格確認書 (card size, under 70) | Nagoya city — https://www.city.nagoya.jp/kurashi/hoken/1011736/1011750/1011753.html (`shikakukakuninsyo_sample2.png`) |

The red circled numbers on the passport images and the みほん stamp on the My Number card are part of the
published images.
